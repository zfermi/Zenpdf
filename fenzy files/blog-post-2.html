10 Hidden Features in Fenzy That Will Transform Your Viewing Experience

You've downloaded Fenzy, you're enjoying unlimited streaming, and you think you know everything about the app. But here's the truth: most users are only scratching the surface of what Fenzy can do. Our app is packed with powerful features designed to enhance your viewing experience—features that many users never discover.

Today, we're pulling back the curtain and revealing 10 hidden features that will completely transform how you watch content on Fenzy. Whether you're a new user or a long-time subscriber, these tips will help you get the absolute most out of your Fenzy experience.

1Custom Video Quality Per Network

Most users know they can adjust video quality in Settings, but did you know Fenzy can automatically adjust quality based on whether you're on WiFi or mobile data?

How to Enable:

Settings → Video Quality → Advanced → Enable "Automatic Quality by Network"

Set your WiFi streaming to 4K for that crisp, cinema-quality experience at home, while automatically switching to 720p on mobile data to save your data plan. Fenzy remembers your preferences and switches automatically—no more manually adjusting settings when you leave the house.

2Continue Watching Across All Devices

Started watching a movie on your phone during your commute? Pick up exactly where you left off on your TV when you get home.

Fenzy's seamless cross-device synchronization works in real-time. Not only does it remember what you were watching, but it syncs your exact timestamp, subtitle preferences, and audio settings. Start on your tablet, finish on your smart TV, and Fenzy handles all the details.

Pro Tip:

Enable "Sync Playback Position" in Settings → General → Sync Settings to ensure your watch position updates instantly across all devices.

3Create Multiple User Profiles

Sharing your Fenzy account with family? Create separate profiles for each person to get personalized recommendations and keep watchlists organized.

Each profile maintains its own:

  • Watch history and progress
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Favorite channels and content
  • Language preferences
  • Parental control settings

This means your teenage daughter's K-drama obsession won't mess up your sports recommendations, and your partner's reality TV binges stay in their own profile.

How to Set Up:

Settings → Account → Manage Profiles → Add Profile

4Advanced Search Filters

Looking for a specific type of content but don't know the title? Fenzy's advanced search goes way beyond basic keyword matching.

You can filter by:

  • Genre combinations: "Action + Comedy + 2020s"
  • IMDB rating: Only show movies rated 7.0 or higher
  • Release year: Find classic films from the 1980s
  • Country of origin: Discover content from specific countries
  • Duration: Filter for movies under 90 minutes when you're short on time
  • Language: Find content in your preferred language

Hidden Trick:

Long-press the search icon to access Quick Filters for instant genre browsing without typing.

5Picture-in-Picture Mode

Need to check a message or browse other apps while watching? Enable Picture-in-Picture (PiP) mode to keep your content playing in a floating window.

This feature is a game-changer for:

  • Watching live sports while browsing game stats
  • Following news broadcasts while checking related articles
  • Keeping an eye on a show while messaging friends about it

The PiP window is resizable, can be positioned anywhere on your screen, and maintains full playback controls. Some users don't even realize this exists—it's become one of our most-loved features once discovered.

How to Activate:

While playing content, tap the PiP icon in the player controls, or simply switch apps and Fenzy will automatically enter PiP mode (if enabled in Settings).

6Scheduled Recordings for Live TV

Yes, you read that right. Even though Fenzy is a streaming service, you can "record" live TV shows to watch later.

This isn't traditional recording—it's even better. When you schedule a recording, Fenzy automatically captures the stream and stores it in your cloud library for up to 7 days. No storage space used on your device, and you can watch from any device on your account.

Perfect for:

  • Never missing live sports events in different time zones
  • Recording news broadcasts to watch at your convenience
  • Saving live concerts and special events

How to Record:

Browse to any live channel → Tap the program → Select "Record" → Choose single episode or series recording.

7Smart Download for Offline Viewing

Traveling somewhere without internet? Fenzy's Smart Download feature automatically downloads episodes of your favorite series when you're on WiFi.

Set it up once, and Fenzy will:

  • Automatically download the next episodes of series you're watching
  • Only download when connected to WiFi (saves your mobile data)
  • Delete watched episodes to free up space
  • Prioritize new releases and trending content
  • Manage storage smartly to never fill up your device

Wake up every morning with fresh content ready to watch offline during your commute. It's like magic, but it's just smart automation.

Setup Location:

Settings → Downloads → Smart Download → Enable and configure preferences

8Custom Subtitle Styling

Default subtitles too small? Wrong color? Fenzy lets you customize every aspect of subtitle appearance:

  • Font size (from tiny to extra large)
  • Font style (multiple typefaces available)
  • Text color and background
  • Outline and shadow effects
  • Position on screen

Users with visual impairments especially appreciate this feature, but it's useful for anyone who wants subtitles that are easy to read without blocking the action on screen.

Access Point:

While playing content: Settings icon → Subtitles → Customize Appearance

9Voice Search (Multilingual)

Typing on a TV remote is painful. That's why Fenzy supports voice search—and not just in English.

Simply press and hold the search button, then speak:

  • "Action movies from the 90s"
  • "Shows similar to Breaking Bad"
  • "Latest Marvel movies"
  • "Spanish language series"

Fenzy understands natural language queries in 12+ languages and can even understand mixed-language requests. Our AI-powered search gets smarter the more you use it, learning your preferences and viewing patterns.

Supported Languages:

English, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, and more being added regularly.

10Parental Controls with Time Limits

Parents, this one's for you. Fenzy's parental controls go beyond simple content ratings—you can set viewing time limits and schedules.

Features include:

  • Content Filtering: Block content by age rating (G, PG, PG-13, R, etc.)
  • Time Limits: Set daily or weekly viewing limits
  • Scheduled Access: Allow viewing only during certain hours (e.g., 4-8 PM on weekdays)
  • PIN Protection: Require PIN for adult content or to override limits
  • Usage Reports: See what your kids are watching and for how long

Create a healthy viewing environment without constant monitoring. The app enforces the rules automatically, removing the need for arguments about screen time.

Configuration:

Settings → Parental Controls → Set PIN → Configure restrictions and time limits
