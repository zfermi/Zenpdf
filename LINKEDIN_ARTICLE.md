- All files processed server-side and deleted within 1 hour

### **Why $3/Month?**

**Transparency matters.** Here's our thinking:

1. **Server costs**: ~$0.50/user/month (Railway hosting, PostgreSQL)
2. **Payment processing**: ~$0.15/transaction (PayPal fees)
3. **Development**: Bootstrapped by a small team
4. **Profit margin**: Minimal – we're focused on growth, not gouging

We believe **affordable doesn't mean inferior**. Our infrastructure is enterprise-grade, our code is clean, and our commitment to users is unwavering.

### **The Technical Stack** (For the Nerds 🤓)

- **Backend**: Python/Flask with PostgreSQL
- **PDF Processing**: PyPDF2, pdf2docx, Pillow
- **Hosting**: Railway (auto-scaling, zero-downtime deploys)
- **Security**: Talisman headers, CSRF protection, rate limiting
- **Analytics**: Server-side GA4 (privacy-respecting)
